Subject: Update: 5 Day Work Week - Effective 15 July 2026

From: BCT-HR4u <BCT-HR4u@bahwancybertek.com>

Date: Wed, 24 Jun 2026 04:54:13 +0000

Dear BCTians,

Over the past few years, we have demonstrated remarkable resilience and adaptability in the way we work. As we continue to evolve as an organization, it is equally important that we strengthen how we collaborate, connect, and build our shared culture, while also driving greater consistency and productivity across our teams.

With this in mind, we will be transitioning to a five-day work week effective 15 July 2026, as part of our return-to-office framework. This step reflects our commitment to fostering stronger teamwork, enabling faster decision-making, and supporting a more productive and engaging workplace for all.

To support this transition, below are a quick set of guidelines to ensure consistency, fairness, and operational effectiveness across the organization:

1. Eligibility

* G5 and above across all functions
* All enabler/support function associates across all grades (including contractors)

2. Mandate Attendance Regularization

Attendance application is mandatory for all working days. Only past and current dates may be regularized. Future dates are not permitted in the tool.

3. Less than 9 Hours Clock-in

Associates must regularize any shortage in completing 9 hours of office clock-in - This is applicable only for less than 2 hours of regularization per day.

4. Client Site Guidelines

Associates working from or visiting client site must mark attendance as ON-SITE while updating their records in EBS

5. Salary Impact

Any days that have not been regularized, or without applied leave will be treated as LOP, and deductions would be made accordingly.

6. Exceptions

Exceptions due to personal, medical, or emergency situations must be proactively communicated and approved by the Reporting Manager, Business Head, and HRBP. Exceptions will only be considered with explicit business and HRBP approval.

Please note that the payroll cut-off date would be 21st of every month and therefore we request you to regularize your attendance on or before 21st of every month.

This transition reinforces consistency, accountability, and how we show up for one another to deliver our best work every day. Your partnership in making this successful is essential.

Please reach out to your Reporting Manager or HRBP team, in case of any questions or support required!

Thank you for your continued commitment and contribution.

Warm Regards,

Team HR
